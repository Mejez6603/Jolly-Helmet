Terminals (the A, B, C boards and the Refilling Station) are plain ESP32 (WROOM) boards on screw-terminal expansion boards. They hold every sensor, relay and display wire. A3 and B3 must not be WROVER (PSRAM) boards, because GPIO 16 and 17 are relays.

Terminals (A, B, C, Refilling Station)

Keep one spare ESP32 per role, flashed and labelled (A1, S3B, Refilling Station...).

Alert banners (red): the Refilling Station offline, a refill that failed, a box out of order, and the two memory and log-storage notes (see Warnings and crash reports).

Refilling Station: the three main containers (EMPTY to MAX), who is waiting, which valve is open and a failed refill. Its settings link opens the Refilling Station page.

How to use it (Box 1 and Box 2 have this section; Box 3 is simpler, see "Box 3 settings" below):

Refill tries (Box 1 and Box 2, older program): the request to a refill station is only sent when that station works. Box 3 has no container sensors of its own: the Refilling Station reads its container. Then tap Send LEVELS.

Box 3 has no phases: a cleaning always runs UV 1, UV 2 and the three humidifiers, and the auto-dry that follows runs UV and the heater (see Box 3 settings).

Box 3 - Drying: set the cool-down seconds, then Send DRYING. The drying time itself is what the customer's coins bought (see Box 3 settings).

Drain pumps (Box 1 only) and the coin power (Box 1 and Box 3) are not part of the 2-second safety. They stay on until you switch them off.

In an emergency, tap CANCEL, then CONFIRM. The machine stops. After the short auto-dry (Box 3 cleaning) or cool-down (dryers) the door unlocks. If you cannot wait, use the safety slide.

If your helmet is stuck after a power cut or a fault, do not pull on the door. In Box 3 the door unlocks by itself and the buzzer beeps while the screen says OUT OF ORDER. In every box you can also slide the safety lock down (lower left of the enclosure door): the door unlocks at once. Then tell the staff.

Box 1 (cleaning): 15 coins = 5 minutes.

Box 3 (cleaning or drying): 15 coins = 5 minutes, and nothing else to choose. INSERT THE REQUIRED COIN asks for the first 15 coins; every extra coin adds 20 seconds, up to 90 coins (30 minutes). The 3-minute auto-dry that ends every cleaning is free.

Stop the run. Touch CANCEL, then CONFIRM. The UV-C, the mist and the heater switch off (Box 3 then dries a cancelled cleaning for 3 minutes with UV light and warm air, and rests for 60 seconds after a cancelled drying; Box 2 cools down with the fan). The coins are not refunded.
