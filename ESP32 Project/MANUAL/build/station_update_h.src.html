There are three Jolly Helmet stations: Box 1, Box 2 and Box 3. They all work the same way. Each one has a touch screen, a coin slot and a door, and each one can clean and dry your helmet.

ModeWhat it does
CLEANINGRemoves odor: UV light plus a fine mist of the cleaning liquid, then 3 minutes of automatic drying (AUTO DRY). Offered only when the cleaning liquid is high enough; otherwise only DRYING.
DRYINGDries a wet helmet with warm air and UV light, then lets it cool down (COOL DOWN) before you take it out.

Tip / Paalala: Sa kahit anong Box, piliin ang CLEANING kung may amoy ang helmet at ang DRYING kung basa ito. Pagkatapos ng CLEANING, awtomatiko nang natutuyo ang helmet mo.

Box 1, Box 2 and Box 3 work the same way, and every one does both jobs. A station cleans (UV light and a fine mist), then dries the helmet, or it only dries. You pay the coins and the machine runs for the time they buy.

A station never keeps a helmet locked in on purpose. If it cannot work, the screen shows OUT OF ORDER. When your helmet is inside and the door is shut, the screen also says "Your helmet is still inside. Take it out, then close the door.", the door unlocks by itself and the buzzer beeps (100 ms on, 500 ms off) until you open the door. During a power cut a backup battery (UPS) keeps the screen, the sensors and the door lock alive, so the same thing happens. If the door stays locked, do not force it: tell the staff. A session that was paid but cut by a power failure is not restored.

Every station (Box 1, Box 2 and Box 3): 15 coins = 5 minutes, and nothing else to choose. INSERT THE REQUIRED COIN asks for the first 15 coins; every extra coin adds 20 seconds, up to 90 coins (30 minutes) in all. The 3-minute auto-dry that ends every cleaning is free.

Do not force the door. It is locked on purpose while the machine runs. To stop, use CANCEL on the screen. If the screen does not answer, tell the staff (see "If the door will not open").

In an emergency, tap CANCEL, then CONFIRM. The machine stops. After the short auto-dry (cleaning) or cool-down (drying) the door unlocks. If you see smoke, smell burning or see sparks, step away and call the staff at once.

If your helmet is stuck after a power cut or a fault, do not pull on the door. The door unlocks by itself and the buzzer beeps while the screen says OUT OF ORDER. Then tell the staff.
