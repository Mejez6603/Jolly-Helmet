A2 (Box 1) and B2 (Box 3) - sensors

  • B2 (Box 3) reads only the helmet ultrasonic (GPIO 26 and 25) and the four door switches (27, 14, 19, 18; the last one is the Side door). The container ultrasonics (32, 33, 23, 22) and the two water sensors (34, 35) are not read by the B2 firmware: the Refilling Station reads the containers.

Box 3 notes: the two drain pump channels (GPIO 33 and 32) are never driven by the B3 firmware, whatever S3B or a dashboard asks (the drain is a manual valve). The lock on GPIO 23 is the SIDE LOCK (it was called the ACS side-door lock).

Refilling Station - sensors and valves

One plain ESP32 (WROOM) on a screw-terminal expansion board, plus a relay module with 3 channels used. The pins are in the pin sheet RevisedJollyHelmet_Checked.xlsx (block F1) and in ESP32_REFILL.ino.

  • The sensors are on ADC1 pins (GPIO 32 to 39) because ADC2 cannot be read while Wi-Fi or ESP-NOW runs. Power the sensors from 3.3 V: the ADC input must never see 5 V.
  • The relay modules of this project are active-LOW (LOW at an input = relay on = valve open). The firmware assumes that (VALVE_ACTIVE_LOW 1); if the module you fit is active-HIGH, set it to 0.
  • First power-up with the valves unplugged: the three relay LEDs must be OFF. The serial banner says which level the sketch assumes. Then use Test valve one valve at a time.
  • The valve coil voltage (12 V DC or 220 V AC) is not decided yet. No flyback diode was asked for (the station stands apart from the boxes and has no coin signal). If the station resets when a valve switches, put a diode across a DC coil or an RC snubber across an AC coil.
  • The relay pins are not strapping pins (0, 2, 5, 12, 15) and not flash pins, and stay quiet at power-up. The valves are driven closed before the pins become outputs.

Every box is a group of ESP32 boards and the Refilling Station is one board. They share only power and the radio channel.
