Box 3 notes: the two spare channels (GPIO 33 and 32) are never driven by the B3 firmware, whatever S3B or a dashboard asks. The lock on GPIO 23 is the SIDE LOCK (the lock of the side door).

The locks are power-to-unlock: the relay is ON only while the door may open (the OPEN steps, Maintenance, or the trapped-helmet release). With no power a solenoid keeps its door locked. The 1N4007 takes the voltage spike when the relay lets go of the coil. Without it the spike can reset the board and burn the relay contacts.

After a power cut the solenoid locks stay shut until the power is back. The UPS on B1, B2, B3 and the lock supply keeps Box 3 able to release a trapped helmet. When power is back, release a lock from the Control Center: Box page, Maintenance, ENCLOSURE LOCK. It switches itself off after 60 seconds.

Hand-set relays in Maintenance switch themselves off after 2 seconds (the box does it by itself; the coin power is exempt, and the tick box Bypass Safety 2 Seconds Relay turns it off). With the bypass the heater still goes off after 5 minutes and the enclosure lock after 60 seconds.

On a phone, join the Wi-Fi JollyHelmet-Online (password 12345678). The setup page opens by itself. If it does not, open http://192.168.4.1. It looks like a router page: the menu button (three bars, top left) opens Network Map, Internet, Blynk and More settings.

Open Internet. Tap Search for networks and tap your Wi-Fi, or type its name (Network name). Type its password (Show lets you check it).

Open Blynk and paste the Blynk device Auth Token. The Public status device token is optional (see Customer status page below).

Open More settings. Choose how often to send (1 minute is a good start), the time zone (8 for the Philippines) and, if you want the quota line on the web page, the number of Blynk messages your plan gives per month. Tick "single numbers V1 to V4" and "Blynk event" only if you made them in Blynk.

Tap Save and connect. The board tests the Wi-Fi and Blynk at once. The setup page may stop answering for a few seconds: this is normal, the board moves to your router's channel. It reconnects by itself and Network Map shows the result.
