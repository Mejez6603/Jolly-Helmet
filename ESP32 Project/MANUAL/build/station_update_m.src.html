INSERT THE REQUIRED COIN: insert coins until the count reaches the number asked (15 coins = 5 minutes). CANCEL takes you back to the start. After the last coin the station waits about 2 seconds so a big coin is counted in full: every coin counts, so with a 20-peso coin for 15 asked, the extra 5 coins add 100 seconds.

I put in a bigger coin than the machine asked for.The station keeps counting for about 2 seconds after the last coin, then every coin counts: the extra coins add time (20 seconds each, up to 90 coins in all).
  • One coin is many pulses (a 20-peso coin is 20 pulses over 1 to 2 seconds) and B1 reports its pulse count to S3B once a second. When the required coins are reached, S3B (Box 3) therefore keeps counting until no new pulse has been seen for 1.5 seconds (at most 6 seconds) and only then moves on: every coin counted becomes time and revenue, up to the most coins (default 90).
