The customer status page shows only whether each machine is free, in use or out of order. The public Blynk device behind it holds nothing else.

Box 3 notes: the two drain pump channels (GPIO 33 and 32) are never driven by the B3 firmware, whatever S3B or a dashboard asks (the drain is a manual valve). The lock on GPIO 23 is the SIDE LOCK (the lock of the side door).
