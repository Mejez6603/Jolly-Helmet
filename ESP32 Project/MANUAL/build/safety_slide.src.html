If the door will not open

The door is locked on purpose while a machine runs. If the screen does not answer, the power is off, or the lock is broken, you can always open the door yourself with the safety slide.

  1. Look at the lower left corner of the enclosure door. The safety slide is there.
  2. Slide it down. The door unlocks at once, with or without power.
  3. Open the door and take your helmet out. Tell the staff afterwards.

Safety slide on the enclosure door

The enclosure door has a mechanical safety slide at its lower left. Sliding it down unlocks the door by hand. It works with no power, with a broken solenoid and without any help from the boards. It is the way out for a trapped helmet.

  • The slide is part of the door. It is not wired and the firmware does not read it. When the door opens, the door sensor reports it: during a run the box shows SAFETY PAUSE and the UV-C, the mist and the heater are switched off within about 2 seconds.
  • A door switch in series with each UV-C lamp (recommended, see Switching 220 V loads) cuts the lamp at once when the door opens, even before the firmware reacts.
  • After the slide has been used, put it back in its normal position, close the door and check that the lock holds it. The Maintenance page of the Control Center switches the lock for a test.
  • Test the slide every month (see the schedule in Maintenance): with the power off, sliding it down must unlock the door by hand. Never block, tape or remove the slide.
