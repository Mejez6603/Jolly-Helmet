Stop the run. Touch CANCEL, then CONFIRM. A cancelled cleaning is dried for 3 minutes with UV light and warm air (AUTO DRY), a cancelled drying rests for 60 seconds (COOL DOWN), then the door unlocks. The coins are not refunded.

A helmet is stuck, or the power is off: do not force the door. Read "A helmet is stuck inside" below.

The solenoid locks are power-to-unlock: with the power off, or with a broken solenoid, they stay locked. Box 3 releases a trapped helmet by itself when it is out of order (see below). There is no mechanical release, so keep the UPS and the lock supply in good order. Staff can unlock the door from the Control Center: box page, Maintenance, ENCLOSURE LOCK.

Fastest way: tap CANCEL, then CONFIRM on the screen. After the short auto-dry (cleaning) or cool-down (drying) the door unlocks. If a door was forced open while the machine was running it pauses (SAFETY PAUSE) and the UV-C, the mist and the heater switch off. The helmet may be warm.

If a terminal hears nothing from its brain for 15 seconds, it switches the heater, UV-C, mist and coin power off.
After drying, everything rests for 60 seconds by default so the helmet can cool (Box 2 also runs its fan). Also after a cancelled drying; a cancelled cleaning goes through its 3-minute auto-dry first.
When a box's container is EMPTY or LOW, or the Refilling Station is silent, only DRYING is offered; the station refills the container by itself.
