Box 3 does both jobs. It cleans (UV light and a fine mist), then dries the helmet, or it only dries. There is nothing to rate and no time to type: you pay the coins and the machine runs for the time they buy.

  1. Tap the screen. For 2 seconds it says Checking Main Container: the machine asks the Refilling Station whether the cleaning liquid is high enough. Tap again at WELCOME.
  2. SELECT MODE: tap CLEANING or DRYING. If CLEANING is grey (SELECT DRY ONLY), the liquid is low or the Refilling Station is not answering: only DRYING can be chosen.
  3. INSERT THE REQUIRED COIN: insert coins until the count reaches the number asked (15 coins = 5 minutes). CANCEL takes you back to the start.
  4. OPEN, PLACE, CLOSE: the door unlocks and the buzzer beeps. Open it, put your helmet in, close the door.
  5. The machine runs for the time your coins bought. CLEANING uses UV light and mist, then AUTO DRY dries the helmet for 3 minutes with UV light and warm air (the warm air stops for the last 30 seconds). DRYING uses warm air and UV light, then COOL DOWN lets the helmet rest for 60 seconds.
  6. OPEN, TAKE, CLOSE: the door unlocks again. Take your helmet out and close the door. The screen says "Thank you for your patronage!".

Adding coins and cancelling

  • You can add coins while a cleaning or a drying is running. Every coin adds time, up to 90 coins (30 minutes) in all. Coins are not taken during AUTO DRY and COOL DOWN.
  • CANCEL (top right) asks "Cancel operation? COIN(s) won't be Refunded." CONFIRM stops the run, RESUME carries on. A cancelled cleaning still goes through the 3-minute AUTO DRY, because the helmet is wet. A cancelled drying goes through the 60-second COOL DOWN. Then the door unlocks.
  • If you open the door while the machine runs, it pauses (SAFETY PAUSE) and switches the UV light and the heat off. Close the door and it carries on.
  • There is no rating and no free retry in Box 3.

If the power goes out or Box 3 shows OUT OF ORDER

Box 3 never keeps a helmet locked in. If it cannot work, the screen shows OUT OF ORDER. When your helmet is inside and the door is shut, the screen also says "Your helmet is still inside. Take it out, then close the door.", the door unlocks by itself and the buzzer beeps (100 ms on, 500 ms off) until you open the door. If the door stays locked, slide the safety lock down (lower left of the door). A session that was paid but cut by a power failure is not restored.

Refilling Station

One small board (the Refilling Station) keeps the main container of every box full. It has three water-level sensors, one in each container, and three valves, one for each box, all fed from one water supply. It works alone: the boxes never ask it for anything. Box 3 already reads it; Box 1 and Box 2 will do the same when they are rebuilt.

How it works

  • Every second the station reads the three sensors and works out each container's level: EMPTY, LOW, MID, HIGH or MAX.
  • A container that is EMPTY or LOW joins the queue. EMPTY containers go first, LOW second; inside a group the one that waited longest goes first.
  • Only one valve is ever open. A refill that is running is never interrupted. The valve closes when the container reaches MAX.
  • If the level does not rise (30 seconds while EMPTY, 5 seconds while LOW, or the valve has been open 3 minutes) the refill FAILED: the valve closes, a flag is set and the station tries again after 60 seconds, for ever.
  • The station tells everybody the levels every second. A box offers CLEANING only when the station was heard in the last 15 seconds and its container is MID or higher; otherwise only DRYING.
  • The water supply has no sensor of its own. An empty supply shows as a refill that does not rise.

Reading the Refilling Station page

  • The top line says whether the station is online, which valve is open and who is waiting.
  • Each box has a card: its level (EMPTY, LOW, MID, HIGH, MAX), a bar, the raw reading with the MID, HIGH and MAX numbers, and a tag: valve OPEN (refilling), waiting for its turn, full, or FAILED.
  • The same three levels sit on the Dashboard under Refilling Station, and on the online page as three dials.

Setting up the levels

  1. Dry sensor: note the raw value on the Live card. Then put the sensor in the real liquid at the level you want to call LOW, MID, HIGH and MAX, and note the raw value each time (it is 0 when dry and grows as the liquid rises).
  2. Type the four edges for that box: Empty up to, MID starts, HIGH starts, MAX starts (they must grow in that order, up to 4095). Defaults: 0, 500, 1000, 1200.
  3. Tap Send Box 1 to the station (or 2, 3). A green tick means the station saved it. Copy Box 1's numbers into Box 2 and Box 3 fills the other forms when the containers are alike.

Test a valve by hand

  • Set how long a test opens the valve (default 2 seconds, 1 to 30), tap Send, then tap Test valve on a container's card. The valve closes by itself.
  • It only works while the station is idle. During a refill the button is greyed out and says busy. A test does not touch the queue or the failed flags.
  • Do the first test with the supply connected and a bucket under the tube, one valve at a time.

When a refill fails

  • The tag FAILED stays on until a refill works, or the container reads MID or higher for 3 seconds (somebody filled it by hand). The Online Server and the web page raise a warning.
  • Check the supply (tank full, tap open), then the decision tree "The Refilling Station does not fill" in the Reference chapter. The station keeps trying every 60 seconds by itself.
  • If the station itself is not heard (OFFLINE on the Dashboard), every box offers DRYING only. That raises a critical alert on the online page.
