Refilling Station: the main container levels (EMPTY to MAX; Box 1 and Box 2 say NOT WATCHED for now), who is waiting, which valve is open and a failed refill. Its settings link opens the Refilling Station page.

One small board (the Refilling Station) keeps the main container full. For now it watches and refills Box 3 only: Box 1 and Box 2 are still being worked on and show NOT WATCHED. The board is built for three containers (three water-level sensors, three valves, one water supply) so Box 1 and Box 2 can be switched on later. It works alone: the boxes never ask it for anything, they only listen to what it reports.

Every second the station reads the sensor of each container it watches (Box 3 for now) and works out the level: EMPTY, LOW, MID, HIGH or MAX.

  • Box 1 and Box 2 are not watched for now. The station reports them as NOT WATCHED (no level, no warning), never queues them, never opens their valves and refuses the manual test of their valves. Their sensors and valves can stay unconnected.

Each box has a card: its level (EMPTY, LOW, MID, HIGH, MAX), a bar, the raw reading with the MID, HIGH and MAX numbers, and a tag: valve OPEN (refilling), waiting for its turn, full, or FAILED. A box the station does not watch says NOT WATCHED and its test button is greyed out.

The same levels sit on the Dashboard under Refilling Station, and on the online page as dials.

Box 1 and Box 2 cannot be tested for now.

Every box has a dial for its main container, read by the Refilling Station. The dial is red when the container is EMPTY or LOW (the box then offers drying only), yellow at MID, yellow-green at HIGH and green at MAX. A small valve symbol above a dial lights blue while the station is filling that container. A box the station does not watch (Box 1 and Box 2 for now) has a grey dial that says NOT WATCHED and raises no alert. So you can see a problem before a customer does.
