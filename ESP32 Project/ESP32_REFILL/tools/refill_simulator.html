Refilling Station simulator

The queue, stall and retry logic of ESP32_REFILL.ino ported line for line to JavaScript, with three simulated containers. It is a test bench for the RULES (who goes first, when a refill fails, what happens when two boxes need liquid), not for the hardware. Time here is virtual: pick a speed.
