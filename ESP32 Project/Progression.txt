Step Procedures
1. INSERT COIN (XX) # to Start Cleaning Process
2. Please put your Headgear inside the Chamber (after Putting the Coin)
3.1a Please Close the Chamber, Don't remove the Headgear. (Open Enclosure Door) (if the Headgear is Detected and the Enclosure Door is Closed goto 4.1a)
3.2a Quote from step 2. (Loops 2 and 3 if the Headgear is not Detected and the Door is Closed)
(4. is just Preparation for the System todo and Measure if the Liquid is enough for the System Humifier)
4.1a Please Wait... > goto 4.1b 
4.1b Preparaing... (0% > 100%) goto 4.1c 
4.1c Starting... goto 5.1a
5.1a Progression: 0% > 100% > goto 6.1 (there is "Abort" Button from the Top Right of the Screen, if the Button is Pressed goto 5.1b)
5.1b Notice: Are you sure you want to Abort the Operation? # You can't Refund your Money. 
5.1bOption YES > goto "6.2" 
5.1bOption NO > Resume the Progression > goto 5.1a with the Progress
6.1 Operation Complete (Selecta Music) > goto 7
6.2 Operation Cancelled (Doom Music) > goto 7
7. You may now Retrieve your Headgear.
8.1a Thank you for your Patronage! # We Hope to See you Again! :D (after Getting the Headgear)
8.1b back to "1"
